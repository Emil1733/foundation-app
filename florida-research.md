# The Florida Sinkhole Project: Cluster-by-Cluster Research

Florida's geology is completely different from Texas or the Midwest. Instead of expansive clay, Florida sits on porous limestone (karst topography). When the water table drops, the limestone dissolves, creating massive underground voids that swallow homes (sinkholes). South Florida also suffers from settlement due to homes built on decomposing organic muck/peat.
